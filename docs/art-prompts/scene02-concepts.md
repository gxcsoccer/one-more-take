# 第 02 场风格设定图提示词

三张图使用内置 ImageGen 生成。每张首稿都由下面的共同描述和对应的单图描述组成。

## 共同描述

Create an art-direction reference for the handmade stop-motion miniature film set in One More Take! Finish the physical prop as a real skilled model maker would: tactile materials, restrained wear at touched edges, careful appealing shapes, simple silhouette. The toy duck design is warm egg-yolk yellow enamel tin, small orange-red bill, two squat orange feet, black bead eyes, a compact brass loop winding key upright behind the neck lower than the crown, embossed oval wing with a rolled seam, a small upturned tail, no wardrobe. The cardboard housing-block design uses warm biscuit cream paper #e6d2b3 with narrow hand-painted stripes at the base of the storeys in muted coral #d98a5f, ochre #e8b85a and sage #7fb5a6. Entire image is textless, free of labels, numbers, branding or watermark. Broad omnidirectional flat fill and restrained material highlights, absolutely no cast shadows, projected silhouettes, shadows painted onto surfaces, light beams, vignette or dramatic directional light.

## 片场全景

Make one immersive wide landscape concept photograph, roughly 3:2, showing the whole small tabletop movie set from a raised three-quarter angle, looking toward a freestanding cream four-storey miniature Singapore public housing block with repeated cross-mullion paper windows, horizontal storey bands and a restrained parapet, against a simple painted deep teal theatrical sky and distant unrecognizable paper blocks. The wooden table edge is visible. Make the staging spatially intelligible: a single red enamel and brass spotlight trolley stays low on a twin rail leading from the foreground toward the building, its lens aimed past the hero duck onto the broad front facade. The squat right-facing duck stands BETWEEN the lamp and facade, side visible; leave clear air around it. An open blank cream-striped dark clapperboard sits to one side. A tiny black wooden cat appears in one window. Prop-scale fasteners, corrugated cuts, handmade surface construction. Keep the composition calm, spare, brightly legible, without lettering, graphics, mobile UI, artwork borders or studio gear beyond the requested props. Lamp is off.

## 发条鸭三视图

Make a wide landscape orthographic model-maker's turnaround sheet depicting exactly three views of ONE identical hero wind-up duck in a row: straight-on FRONT looking at camera, precise right-facing SIDE PROFILE, straight-on BACK facing directly away. Plain single matte pale blue-grey seamless background across the entire sheet; no lines, supports, shadows, borders, lettering or scales. Align their feet on one invisible baseline and crowns on another; consistent orthographic scale, identical key, feet, beak and wing construction, no perspective rotation on front and rear. The side profile should read as approximately 20 cm bill-to-tail by 16 cm top-to-toe, round elevated head, flattened rounded bill at 70 percent of height projecting farthest forward, oval low body with very short squat feet tucked underneath, small upward tail, simple dark polished eyes. Winding key is on top/back centre of body and remains below head top. Slightly polished, yellow painted sheet metal, pressed seams, gently curved specular material reflection; charming and sturdy, impeccably photographed under flat even fill.

## 纸板楼材质特写

One straight-on, front-elevation macro detail photograph of the hand-built four-storey housing facade (only architecture, no duck or other props). Crop close enough that individual warm cardstock fibers, edge thickness, tiny corrugated flutes at an exposed cut on a ledge, and the dry-brush edge of the thin horizontal hand-painted floor bands in muted coral, ochre and sage are unambiguously legible. Fill the frame with a section of the cream façade across multiple levels, disciplined repeated miniature cross-mullion window openings and paper sills with unlit dark teal backing, level horizontals and verticals, no vanishing point. Precise craft, subtle natural imperfections, simple architectural rhythm, friendly Singapore public housing character without recognizable landmarks. Flat neutral material-study light, no cast shadow contours, light shafts, residents, signs, decoration or arbitrary surrounding tools. Landscape 3:2 sheet.

## 全景修订

先以片场首稿和发条鸭三视图为参考，抬高机位，使全景中的鸭子与三视图一致。

Re-stage the existing tabletop concept from image 1 into a clear high three-quarter overhead view. Use image 2 as the reference for the SAME duck's identity: take its twin-hole brass key, yellow pressed-tin oval wing, low feet and broad orange beak. Preserve the miniature craftsmanship and material palette. This change of camera is essential: position the camera looking down from about 40 degrees above the tabletop, offset to one side, so the tops of the trolley, building roof, clapperboard slat, and a substantial area of tabletop are visible. Give the stage room around every edge. The camera must see the tabletop geometry like a child looking down into a shoebox diorama: at the back a four-storey cream paper housing block (four actual rows of small windows, not an empty floor), two visible sides and a visible roof; at the front a small red spotlight trolley on a twin brass rail aligned from near foreground straight TOWARD the facade; halfway along that line of sight, a right-facing yellow wind-up duck OFF the end of the rails and between the lamp and building. Aim the lamp lens toward the duck/building, not at the camera. Blanket-soft even exposure, empty unlit lens with no beam, absolutely no cast shadow silhouettes. A tiny jointed black cat at a window; a striped BLANK clapperboard off to the side, not in front of the duck. Teal handpainted sky panel at the back. One photograph, no insets, no printed caption.
